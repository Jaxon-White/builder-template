# Usability test: 2026-10-15

**Who:** People from DI who help people with needs and use WSRS resource information
**Task I gave them:** Not recorded. We met to talk about the app and they reviewed it.

## What they did

They reviewed the live app and gave this feedback:

- Add counties and states to the "search by name, city, or need" option.
- Mark whether each resource/organization has been vetted, so users can tell at a glance.
- Restore the detailed information that WSRS provides for each resource, so missionaries have
  what they need to help the person they are meeting or speaking with. The current view is too
  minimal and is a step backward.
- Correct the SNAP eligibility wording. "Anyone can apply" is misleading. Show the actual
  qualifiers defined in WSRS.
- Check why the AI search returned the "anyone can apply" statement and fix that source.
- Add a Print button for resource information, alongside the existing Directions button.

## Where they got stuck

- **Not enough detail.** They compared the app with WSRS, which they already use, and found the
  place pages too thin to help someone.
- **Wrong eligibility.** The SNAP listing said "Anyone can apply," which isn't how SNAP works.
  The line was written during AI-assisted research; the 211 listing it cites says nothing about
  eligibility (decision 005).
- **Can't tell what's trustworthy.** Nothing showed at a glance whether a place had been vetted.

## What I am changing because of this

All six, in spec 005. I don't have WSRS access yet, so details and qualifiers come from each
program's official pages, with every fact linked to its source, until WSRS data is available.
