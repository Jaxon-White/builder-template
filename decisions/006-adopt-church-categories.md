# Decision 006: Adopted church HQ's 19 categories

**Date:** 2026-10-29
**Status:** Active

## Context

Church HQ shared its category list (19 categories with subcategories), built from what its
offices already use and changes agreed in a Manager's Meeting and by HQ staff. The app had its
own 8 categories, chosen before talking to anyone at HQ.

## Options considered

1. **Keep the app's 8 categories**: simple, tuned to homelessness / doesn't match HQ's tools or
   language, and has no home for housing, utilities, legal or reentry help.
2. **Adopt HQ's 19 categories and subcategories at once**: full match / bigger change, and
   subcategories have open overlaps (Emergency shelter under Housing and Homeless Services).
3. **Adopt HQ's 19 categories now, subcategories next**: matches HQ's language now, keeps this
   change small, and leaves time to ask HQ about the overlaps.

## Decision

Option 3. Deciding reason: speaking HQ's language is how the app builds on what HQ already
has (Sprint 4 goal), and the categories are settled while the subcategories still have open
questions.

Smaller choices made with it:
- Shelters go under Homeless Services, not Housing, since homeless people are who the app serves.
- Intake shows 7 urgent needs with the rest behind "More needs". All 19 at once was
  overwhelming. Veterans, Seniors, Reentry and approved disability are asked as situations
  instead, since they describe who the person is. Disability is asked as "approved for SSI or
  SSDI", because many programs require official approval, not just a disability.
- "Who it serves" groups stay alongside the Veterans, Seniors, Disability, Domestic Violence
  and Reentry categories, matching HQ's note that those categories are for programs restricted
  to that group.

## What would change our mind

HQ changing its list (Financial Assistance's final decision is still pending), or leaders in
testing missing a need hidden under "More needs", which would mean moving it up.
