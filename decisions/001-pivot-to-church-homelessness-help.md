# Decision 001: Pivoted from consulting billing to helping church leaders find help for homeless people

**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Status:** Active

## Context

Sprint 1 set out to talk to 10 consulting companies about billing and time-tracking pain, then
decide whether to validate or pivot. I stopped after 6 conversations (project managers and a
CFO at local software consulting firms, 20 to 250 people) because no one cared about the
product. Most already had well-integrated billing and time-tracking tools and weren't looking
to change. See `discovery/interviews/001-consulting-round-sep-2026.md`.

## Options considered

1. **Keep going on consulting billing and time tracking**: pros: already scoped, 6 interviews
   done / cons: those interviews showed little pain and no appetite to switch tools.
2. **Consulting firms' trouble dealing with customers who complain**: pros: the one complaint
   that came up most / cons: there's no way to fix how customers behave, and what they
   complain about wasn't recorded.
3. **A tool that helps church leaders find help for homeless people**: pros: a professor
   approached me with it and is helping, and I'm assuming the church is looking for this app
   / cons: that demand is an assumption, not confirmed by the church or its leaders. No conversations with church leaders
   yet.

## Decision

Pivot to helping church leaders find help for homeless people. Deciding reason: the billing
interviews showed that the people I'd sell to don't have the problem.

## What would change our mind

Early conversations with church leaders show they already have a reliable
way to find help for homeless people, or that it isn't a problem they want to solve.
