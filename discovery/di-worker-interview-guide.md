# Interview guide: DI workers (pilot)

**For:** the 3 DI workers in the Sprint 2 pilot. Talk before they try the app, then watch them
use it (log that as a usability test in `design/usability-tests/`).

**Goal:** find out whether helping someone find shelter, food or care is a real, frequent
problem for them, and what they do today. Record their words, not my summary.

## Before showing the app (10 minutes)

1. Tell me about the last time someone at DI needed help beyond what DI offers. What happened?
2. What did you do next? Who did you call or where did you look?
3. How long did that take? Did it work?
4. How often does something like that happen? (This week? This month?)
5. What's the hardest part: knowing where to send them, whether the place is open, whether
   they qualify, or something else?
6. Is there anything you wish you had in that moment?

## Watching them use it (10 minutes)

Task, in their words: "Someone just told you they have nowhere to sleep tonight and they have
two kids with them. Use this to find them somewhere."

- Don't help. Note where they pause, what they tap that I didn't expect, what they say.
- Afterward: "What would you actually do with what it showed you?"

## Wrap-up

- Would you use this the next time? What would stop you?
- Who else at DI deals with this?

After each interview: write `discovery/interviews/NNN-di-worker-....md` from the template and
update `personas.md` and `insights.md` in the same commit.
