# TSO Resource & AI discussion

**Date:** 2026-11-05 (Thursday)
**Format:** In person
**Who:** TSO leadership and HQ staff, a senior missionary, and me. Names left out (public repo).
The notes were written up for a TSO leader afterward.

## Key ideas (from the meeting notes, names removed)

- **Two ways to find resources:** keep the category grid for browsing, and add AI-assisted
  targeted search for a patron's specific circumstances.
- **Targeted search** on things like language, family circumstances, disability, ZIP code and
  immediate need.
- **Search a trusted resource pool** whenever possible, so results are current, vetted,
  appropriate, and aligned with TSO and Church objectives.
- **Start with a standard framework** of roughly 16 to 18 categories with several strong
  resources in each, then let local offices or stakes add resources and subcategories.
- **Possibly several AI agents:** one to build the first resource pool, another to keep
  discovering, updating and expanding it.
- **Use CHAS information** to find Church-supported organizations and favor established partners
  when it fits.
- **Technology supports relationships, it doesn't replace them.** AI surfaces information;
  missionaries verify availability, build provider relationships, and use judgment and spiritual
  guidance. A green "vetted" or yellow "call first" indicator could show reliability.
- **Share across offices** so one office can use resources another already found or vetted.
- **Finalize categories and subcategories before more data entry.** AI can then help move
  existing resources over, with people reviewing uncertain classifications.

## Bottom line

- Leadership needs to finalize the "Key Categories". Each office can create unlimited
  subcategories.
- An HQ staff member proposed: beta test the Resource Tool with the final categories, loaded with
  the top 3 resources in each. Take feedback daily and meet weekly with a working group (a senior
  missionary, a missionary, **me**, and a manager) to start optimizing. Present changes to the
  full Leadership Team periodically for feedback.
